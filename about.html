About Us

Born from Nature, Built by Athlete

Grit & Grains is a small-batch, honest-nutrition brand founded by an avid runner who couldn't find snacks clean enough for her own training table.

Why We Exist

Our Story

Grit & Grains was born from love, loss, and a mother's simple belief that food is the purest way to care for people.

In the final week of my mother's life in December 2022, she asked me to help her make laddus to serve family and friends. It was the first time I had ever made them. I didn't know then that those moments would become some of my most treasured memories.

The following Diwali, missing her deeply, I returned to the kitchen. I recreated those laddus in my own way—made with wholesome ingredients and naturally sweetened, without refined sugar or jaggery. What began as a tribute soon became an annual tradition.

Every winter, I experimented, refined recipes, and shared them with friends. They kept coming back for more, and I realized the best way to carry forward my mother's spirit of generosity was to create something that could reach beyond my own kitchen.

And so, in 2025, Grit & Grains was born.

Today, every bite reflects the values she lived by—care, nourishment, and bringing people together through food. This isn't just our story; it's the love behind everything we make.

🏃‍♀️
Minal Gupta Founder, Grit & Grains
Our Mission

When I couldn't find clean, honest nutrition, I decided to make it.

Between long training runs and Hyrox race prep, I kept hitting the same wall: every "healthy" snack on the shelf had sugar, gur, or some preservative buried in the fine print. So I started making my own — dates, nuts, ragi, peanuts and grit, nothing else.

What began as fuel for my own long runs turned into Grit & Grains: a small, honest kitchen brand that believes healthy can genuinely be tasty.

🚫
